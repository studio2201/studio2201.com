01 · IN PRODUCTION · ANONYMIZED CASE STUDY

Automated Vehicle Telemetry & Recall Sentinel.

Instead of relying on vague warning lights or guesswork, we wired AI directly into live vehicle diagnostic hardware and automated federal recall feeds across modern vehicles.

Step 01 · Wire-Level Capture

Read raw telemetry directly from onboard controllers

Connects over OBD-II, CAN bus, UDS, and KWP protocols across 16 to 31 electronic control units — capturing live sensors, oil life, tire pressures, brake wear, fuel trims, and fault histories.

Step 02 · Automated Sentinels

Cross-check federal recalls, bulletins & warranties weekly

Scheduled workflows monitor NHTSA recall databases, technical service bulletins (TSBs), and extended manufacturer warranty programs against each vehicle's exact configuration.

Step 03 · Plain-English Ledger

Turn raw hex frames into prioritized maintenance actions

Translates raw diagnostic bytes and fault codes into a clear health dashboard, prioritized repair steps, exact OEM replacement part numbers, and verified torque specs.

Real-world outcome: Identified a drifting wideband oxygen sensor before breakdown with exact OEM replacement part numbers, and automatically uncovered over 100,000 miles of remaining extended manufacturer warranty coverage on an electronic control module.
