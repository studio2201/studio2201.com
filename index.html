01

Five products. One substrate. Apache-2.0.

Each product is a thin slice on top of openOODA — the sovereign, capability-aware, post-quantum-signed substrate for AI-era software. The substrate is the engine. The products are how it ships.

v0.1

Vigil

Supply-chain dormancy scanner. Reads your manifest (package.json, Cargo.lock, pyproject.toml, …), scores each dependency against the Necrometer/Seance fate table, emits SUPPLY-CHAIN.md and a badge.

Proven

PQC-signed supply-chain attestor. Merkle AST + ML-DSA-65 + opm. SLSA L3+ on openOODA. Air-gappable, reproducible, post-2030 valid.

Aegis

PQC migration SDK. Scans RSA/ECC usage, plans in OMB M-26-15 format, emits ML-KEM-768 / ML-DSA-65 replacements. Targets the Dec 31, 2030 federal deadline.

Snip
